## Competition Introduction

Built on the BigQuant AI investment research platform, the competition uses real data, open challenges, and professional judging to discover a new generation of fintech talent with AI investment research, quantitative modeling, and strategy innovation capabilities.

### About BigQuant

BigQuant is a leading domestic AI quantitative investment platform with the mission to "Democratize AI to empower investors". As the industry's first platform-level product to systematically apply AI technology to investment, BigQuant deeply integrates frontier AI techniques such as machine learning and deep learning with quantitative investing, providing one-stop solutions covering data, research, backtesting, and live trading for individual quants, research institutions, brokerages, and asset managers. The platform is currently used by multiple leading financial institutions as a foundation for quantitative research and investment decision-making.

The platform's core capabilities span four layers:

* **Data Foundation**: PB-scale standardized financial data plus novel alternative data, covering market quotes, financials, sentiment, supply chains, and other dimensions, with infrastructure for PIT processing and cross-frequency alignment.
* **Factors and Algorithms**: 2000+ built-in base factors, with support for expression engines, UDFs, and AI-driven automated factor mining; integrated AutoML, hyperparameter optimization, rolling training, portfolio optimization, and attribution analysis components.
* **Development Environment**: The DAI data engine delivers high-performance factor computation (faster speed, lower memory footprint); modular visual development integrates seamlessly with Python and Notebooks, balancing low entry barrier with flexibility.
* **Ecosystem and Deployment**: A complete loop from learning to deployment, including a strategy source library, brokerage research reports, the Quant Academy training system, and paper-trading and live-trading integrations.

This track is hosted on BigQuant's real data and development environment. Participants will use the same toolchain as institutional clients to complete factor mining and submission.

### Why Open Innovation

In modern quantitative investment, AI is evolving from "auxiliary tool" to "core productivity". From hedging risk around macro events, to real-time parsing of multimodal information, to end-to-end automatic strategy generation, AI lets quantitative research move beyond the traditional boundaries of Alpha factor mining. For BigQuant, continuously advancing AI deployment across all quantitative scenarios is key to maintaining industry leadership.

This competition is an **open innovation and entrepreneurship project for AI × quantitative investing**: we do not predefine fixed problem statements, nor score daily on a leaderboard. Instead, we invite university talent worldwide to freely propose projects around "innovative AI applications in quantitative scenarios", with judging and ecosystem partnerships completed through offline roadshows. The competition is the entry point; the supporting talent network, incubation resources, and partnership opportunities are the real exit.

## Track Setup

The competition does not enforce a fixed track. Participants can freely propose projects around any of the directions below, or combine multiple directions (just indicate the primary one when submitting):

* **AI Factors and Strategies**: Use LLMs, time-series models, reinforcement learning, and other AI techniques to mine differentiated factors, or build end-to-end AI quantitative strategies.
* **Macro and Event-Driven**: Risk identification, scenario simulation, and hedging schemes around macro events such as Fed meetings, geopolitical events, and policy changes.
* **Multimodal Financial Understanding**: Multimodal parsing and signal generation from heterogeneous financial information including research reports, earnings calls, news, and charts.
* **AI Tools and Infrastructure**: Toolchain innovations for quantitative R&D itself, such as evaluation frameworks, backtest acceleration, interpretability, and Agent workflows.

> Original projects are encouraged. Whether one of the directions above or any "AI innovation related to quant", anything with sound logic and substantial AI application qualifies.

## Data and Tooling

* **Structured Data**: A-share daily and minute-level market data, PIT-processed financial data, and macro indicators.
* **Unstructured Data**: Sentiment corpora including research reports, news, earnings calls, and stock forums (compliance and de-identification applied).
* **Compute**: CPU/GPU Notebook environments. Finalists receive dedicated GPU compute packages during the finals.
* **AI Toolchain**: Open access to mainstream open-source LLM inference APIs and BigQuant's proprietary quantitative foundation model API; built-in modules for backtesting, attribution, risk evaluation, and factor analysis.

## Submission Eligibility Check

Initial submissions after registration must pass the following checks; otherwise they will not enter the finals review pool:

* **Content Relevance**: The submission must be strongly related to the quantitative domain and must not violate financial regulations or public morals.
* **AI Centrality**: The core logic of the submission must be driven by AI and clearly traceable in the code. "AI as a label" is prohibited.
* **Material Completeness**: The Notebook, project description, and AI application description must all be complete and runnable for automatic reproduction in the platform environment.
* **Compliance and Security**: External network access and unauthorized data use are prohibited; LLM outputs must include fact-checking and disclaimers.

> Passing the eligibility check is a prerequisite for participating in the finals roadshow. Submissions that fail will not enter the review pool.

**Advancement Rules**

Each offline grand final has **12 advancement slots**. The organizing committee evaluates preliminary performance across the three competitions (AI Factor Mining, End-to-End Large Models, AI Open Innovation):

* **Direct Advancement**: The **top 2** teams from the preliminary round of each competition advance directly (3 competitions × 2 = 6 slots);
* **Comprehensive Evaluation**: The remaining **6 slots** are selected by the organizing committee based on comprehensive evaluation across the three competitions.

**Submission Materials**

Finalist teams must submit a factor research report on time (PDF, 10–15 pages). The report should be well-structured and rigorously argued, including the following sections:

| Section | Key Content |
|---|---|
| Abstract | Research objectives, core methodology, and main conclusions |
| Track Label | Clearly identify "Traditional" or "AI" track as the participation label |
| Introduction | Factor research background and literature review |
| Factor Construction | Data preprocessing, factor formulas and logic (the AI track must additionally describe the AI application steps) |
| Empirical Analysis | Factor analysis backtest results, performance attribution, robustness tests (performance across market cycles and industries) |
| Innovation and Limitations | Discussion of methodological innovations and limitations; the AI track must describe the innovations in AI application |
| Conclusion | Research summary and future directions |

**Defense and Awards**

The finals defense uses a **"25-minute presentation + 5-minute Q&A with judges"** format, held offline with simultaneous live streaming. Final rankings are announced after the defense, followed by an awards ceremony attended by guests, judges, and participants together.

## Finals Evaluation

Finalist teams will be scored comprehensively at the roadshow by the review committee (composed of BigQuant, institutional buy-side representatives, and investors), with the following criteria:

* **AI Application Innovation and Scenario Fit** (35%):
    * Fit between AI technology choices and quantitative scenarios; effectiveness in solving the problem.
    * Originality in technology combination, model architecture, and application logic.
* **Project Completeness and Reproducibility** (25%):
    * Rigor of data processing, model training, and effect verification workflows.
    * Code quality, reproducibility, and transparency in model tuning and training.
* **Commercialization and Ecosystem Value** (20%):
    * Clarity of target customer profile, willingness-to-pay assumptions, and deployment path.
    * Synergy with the BigQuant ecosystem (data services, strategy marketplace, institutional partnerships).
* **On-Site Performance** (20%):
    * Presentation: depth of understanding of how AI integrates with quantitative scenarios; clarity in conveying innovation and commercial value.
    * Q&A: accuracy in answering judges' questions on AI techniques, application details, and deployment paths.

> Scoring uses a 100-point scale. Multiple judges score independently, and the average is taken to rank teams in descending order.

## Submission Files

During the project development phase, participants submit the following three deliverables (not strictly mandatory):

1. **Core Notebook**: Place the core logic of the AI application in a `main` function, and add the comment tag `# [AI-CORE]` at key AI steps (e.g., LLM calls, model training, automated sentiment analysis).
2. **Project Description Document**: Embedded in the Notebook in markdown format, including scenario definition, data usage, AI approach, effect verification, and commercialization assumptions; if an LLM is used, attach the prompt, model version, and key hyperparameters.
3. **AI Application Description Table**: Item-by-item listing of where AI is involved, the model used, inputs and outputs, and replaceability assessment.

The data returned by the `main` function should clearly reflect the core results of the AI application. Reference format below (adjust based on direction):

| Scenario Type | Core Result Dimension | Result Value |
|---|---|---|
| Fed meeting keyword prediction | Rate hike probability | 0.85 |
| Trump sentiment risk hedging | Hedge portfolio weights | Stock A: 0.3 |
| AI-mined factor | Factor value (per stock) | 000001.SZ: 0.05 |
| Multimodal research report understanding | Key viewpoint extraction | Structured JSON output |
| ... | ... | ... |
